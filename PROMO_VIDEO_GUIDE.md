# 🎬 MagazineForge — Step-by-Step Promo Video Masterclass Guide

This guide provides an end-to-end, zero-cost production workflow for creating a commercial-grade **60-second promo video** for **MagazineForge**.

---

## 🛠️ Free Tool Stack

| Phase | Tool | Function | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Recording** | Android Screen Recorder or `scrcpy` (PC) | Capture high-FPS phone footage | Free |
| **Voiceover** | **ElevenLabs.io** (Adam/Rachel Voice) | Professional AI narration | Free Tier |
| **Editing** | **CapCut Desktop** or **Google Vids** | Assembly, zoom effects & text captions | Free |

## 📱 Phase 1: Phone Recording Setup (5 Minutes)

### 1. Configure Android Device
1. Open **Settings -> Developer Options**:
   - Enable **Show Taps** / **Show Touches** (visualizes finger taps on screen).
2. Set display resolution to **1080p** or **4K** with 90Hz/120Hz refresh rate enabled.
3. Turn on **Do Not Disturb** to block incoming notifications.

### 2. Shot Checklist (Record each clip for 8–10 seconds)
- [ ] **Shot A (Splash Screen)**: Launch MagazineForge -> splash screen transition to Home.
- [ ] **Shot B (Intent Refinement)**: Open Intent Card -> type `"Tokyo Cyberpunk Culture"` -> tap **✨ Refine Prompt** -> show real-time text expansion.
- [ ] **Shot C (Co-Author Swatches)**: Open Co-Author Schema -> expand Cover section -> tap **Classic Gold (#FFD700)** color swatch -> watch title update.
- [ ] **Shot D (Dual Image Picker)**: Tap 🔍 search icon -> scroll through 20+ candidate stock photos from Pixabay & Pexels -> select photo -> show instant live thumbnail preview box.
- [ ] **Shot E (One-Tap Compilation)**: Tap **Compile Magazine** -> show real-time parallel progress bars.
- [ ] **Shot F (The PDF Reveal)**: Open compiled PDF -> scroll through full-bleed cover, Vogue-style Table of Contents (`01`, `02`), and 2-column article spreads.

## 🎙️ Phase 2: AI Voiceover & Audio Script (3 Minutes)

### 1. ElevenLabs Setup
1. Go to [ElevenLabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io).
2. Select Voice: **"Adam"** (Deep, energetic tech voice) or **"Rachel"** (Clean, modern editorial voice).
3. Set Voice Settings: **Stability 50%**, **Clarity 75%**.

### 2. Word-for-Word Timed Narration Script

```text
[0:00 - 0:08]
What if you could turn a single prompt into a print-ready, 16-page luxury magazine in under 60 seconds?

[0:08 - 0:18]
Meet MagazineForge. Type your topic idea, tap Refine Prompt, and watch AI expand your intent into a structured editorial concept in real-time.

[0:18 - 0:32]
You're in total control. Customize title color swatches with one tap, search over 20 stock photos across Pixabay and Pexels, or upload your own images with instant live previews.

[0:32 - 0:42]
With one tap, the automated Python and LaTeX compilation engine balances your typography, builds multi-column layouts, and renders your magazine issue.

[0:42 - 0:54]
The result? A publication-ready PDF document complete with full-bleed covers, structured contents, and high-fashion spreads.

[0:54 - 1:00]
MagazineForge. Turn your ideas into luxury digital and print publications today.
```

## ✂️ Phase 3: Video Assembly & Editing (10 Minutes)

### Step 1: Track Assembly in CapCut or Google Vids
1. Import all 6 screen recording clips + the ElevenLabs `.mp3` voiceover track.
2. Drag the voiceover onto the primary audio track.
3. Trim each screen recording clip to align exactly with the corresponding voiceover timestamp.

### Step 2: Add Smooth Zoom Keyframes (The "Pro Polish")
Whenever the user interacts with key UI features, add a **1.15x Zoom Keyframe**:
- **Zoom In** on the ✨ **Refine Prompt** button right before tapping.
- **Zoom In** on the **Color Swatches** when tapping the gold circle.
- **Zoom In** on the **Image Search Grid** when selecting a photo.

### Step 3: Add Kinetic Text Captions
1. Add bold, modern text overlays (Font: *Montserrat* / *Inter*, Color: Gold/White):
   - `0:00` -> **⚡ Prompt to Magazine in 60 Seconds**
   - `0:08` -> **✨ Real-Time Prompt Refinement**
   - `0:18` -> **🎨 Color Swatches & 20+ Stock Photos**
   - `0:32` -> **⚙️ Automated XeLaTeX Engine**
   - `0:42` -> **📖 Publication-Ready PDF Output**

### Step 4: Add Background Music & Mix Audio
1. Download a background track from Pixabay Audio (Search: *"Cyberpunk Ambient"* or *"Luxury Lo-Fi"*).
2. Set background music volume to **-22 dB** (ducks under voiceover so narration is crystal clear).
